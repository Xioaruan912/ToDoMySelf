# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in PaPerMySelf to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from the experience.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's data, credentials, logs or private correspondence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory comments, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption of discussions are not tolerated. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and with proportional consequences.

## Reporting

Report abusive behavior privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue about a conduct report. Reports are reviewed as promptly as practical, kept confidential to the extent possible, and the reporter's safety and privacy come first.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at the link above.
